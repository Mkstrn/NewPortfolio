Accessible design is good design...

Let's make the world inclusive together!

Hello! My name's Micayla!

I spent four years working towards my bachelor's degree at Michigan State University. With three years of design experience, I strive to make the world a more inclusive space for all types of people.

I believe that people deserve intentional design that works with them, not against them. My design philosophy is simple: "Accessible design is good design."

I've had the opportunity to work across a variety of design environments, with some of my most notable experiences being Constellation Cat Cafe and MSU Athletics. Through these experiences, I collaborated with teams to improve the quality of digital experiences and make navigating websites and online systems more intuitive and accessible.
