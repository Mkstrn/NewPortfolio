Accessible Design is Good Design...

Let's make the world inclusive together!

Hello! My name's Micayla!

I spent four years working towards my bachelors at Michigan State University. With 3 years of design experience, I strive to make the world a more inclusive space for all types of people.

I believe that people deserve intentional design that is working with them, not against them. My design philosophy is simple, “accessible design is good design.”

Ive worked with a variety of spaces with the most notable being Constellation Cat Cafe and MSU Athletics where I worked with a team to improve the quality of life when navigating the systems these websites had in place! MAKE THIS LONGER
